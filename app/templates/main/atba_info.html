About the American Tending Breeds Association

What is ATBA?

The American Tending Breeds Association (ATBA) is a non-profit organization dedicated to preserving and promoting the herding instinct and working ability of tending breeds. We host AKC and AHBA sanctioned herding trials as well as fun runs and educational events throughout the year.

What Are Tending Breeds?

Tending breeds are herding dogs that work in a distinctive style — tending flocks at the boundaries of unfenced land, keeping livestock within a defined area without the use of fences. Breeds include the German Shepherd Dog, Belgian Tervuren, Belgian Malinois, Belgian Sheepdog, Briard, and others.

Herding Titles

AKC Herding

AKC herding trials test a dog's ability to herd livestock under the direction of a handler. Classes include Started, Intermediate, and Advanced. Events may use ducks, sheep, or cattle.

AHBA Herding

The American Herding Breed Association (AHBA) offers its own title program. Classes run I, II, and III. ATBA often runs AKC and AHBA trials in the same weekend.

Membership

ATBA membership is per calendar year. Members receive discounted entry rates for Fun Runs and Smart Dog Days during the pre-entry window. To join or renew, contact us at info@atba-herding.org.
